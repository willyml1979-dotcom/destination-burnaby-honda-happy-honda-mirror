The 2021 Lexus UX 250h is powered by a 2.0-litre 4-cylinder hybrid powertrain producing 181 net horsepower, paired with an electronically controlled continuously variable transmission (eCVT) and Lexus Electronic All-Wheel Drive (eAWD). On the exterior, the 2021 Lexus UX 250h features LED headlights, LED daytime running lights, LED taillights, heated power-folding exterior mirrors with integrated turn signals, rain-sensing windshield wipers, a power moonroof, and 18-inch aluminum-alloy wheels. Inside, the 2021 Lexus UX 250h features NuLuxe-trimmed seating surfaces, heated and ventilated front seats, a heated steering wheel, dual-zone automatic climate control, Lexus Display Audio with a 7-inch touchscreen, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility, Bluetooth connectivity, Smart Key System with push-button start, and a 60/40 split-folding rear seat. The Canadian Premium model also includes eight-way power-adjustable front seats and Lexus Display Audio with Remote. Safety systems on the 2021 Lexus UX 250h include Lexus Safety System+ 2.0 with Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection, Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist, Lane Tracing Assist, All-Speed Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Road Sign Assist, Automatic High Beams, Blind Spot Monitoring, Rear Cross Traffic Alert, Vehicle Stability Control, Traction Control, and a backup camera. The 2021 Lexus UX 250h delivers an excellent balance of luxury, efficiency, and year-round all-wheel-drive confidence.
